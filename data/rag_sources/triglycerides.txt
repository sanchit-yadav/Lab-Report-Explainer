What the Test Measures:
Triglycerides are the predominant form of dietary fat stored in adipose tissue and circulated for cellular energy.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
High triglycerides (>150 mg/dL) are strongly linked to metabolic syndrome, insulin resistance, type 2 diabetes, alcohol excess, and cardiovascular disease. Severely high levels (>500 mg/dL) carry an increased risk of acute pancreatitis.

What Low Values Mean:
Low triglycerides can occur in malnutrition, very low-fat diets, or hyperthyroidism.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- How much do my dietary carbohydrates and sugar intake influence my triglyceride count?
- Was my fasting interval sufficient before this blood draw?
