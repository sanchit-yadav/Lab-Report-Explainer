What the Test Measures:
The RBC count measures the total number of circulating oxygen-carrying erythrocytes in a cubic millimeter of blood.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
Elevated RBC counts (erythrocytosis) are seen in chronic hypoxia, sleep apnea, smoking, dehydration, or primary erythrocytosis (polycythemia vera).

What Low Values Mean:
Low RBC counts indicate anemia resulting from blood loss, nutritional deficiencies, hemolysis, or reduced erythropoietin production in renal disease.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does my RBC level align with my hemoglobin and hematocrit results?
- Are there dietary or lifestyle factors impacting my red blood cell production?
