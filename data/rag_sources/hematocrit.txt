What the Test Measures:
Hematocrit (Hct) represents the proportion of total blood volume that consists of packed red blood cells, expressed as a percentage.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
Elevated hematocrit often reflects hemoconcentration due to dehydration, but can also signify chronic hypoxia or polycythemia.

What Low Values Mean:
A low hematocrit is a primary hallmark of anemia or recent fluid overload.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Was my hydration status a factor in this hematocrit reading?
- How does this compare with previous blood counts?
