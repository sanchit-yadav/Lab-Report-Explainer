What the Test Measures:
HbA1c reflects your average blood sugar levels over the preceding 2 to 3 months by measuring the percentage of hemoglobin molecules in red blood cells coated with glucose.

What Diagnostic Thresholds Mean:
Normal: Below 5.7%.
Prediabetes: 5.7% to 6.4%.
Diabetes: 6.5% or higher on two separate occasions.
In diagnosed diabetics, individual target goals (often <7.0%) are customized with a physician.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does my HbA1c indicate prediabetes or diabetes?
- What individualized target HbA1c should I aim for over the next 3 months?
- How often should I recheck my HbA1c?
