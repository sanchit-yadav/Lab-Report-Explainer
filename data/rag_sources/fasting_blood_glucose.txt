What the Test Measures:
Measures circulating blood sugar concentration after an overnight fast of at least 8 to 12 hours.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
Fasting glucose between 100–125 mg/dL indicates impaired fasting glucose (prediabetes). Levels ≥126 mg/dL on repeat testing indicate diabetes mellitus. Acute stress, steroids, and pancreatitis can also elevate glucose.

What Low Values Mean:
Low blood glucose (hypoglycemia, <70 mg/dL) causes dizziness, shakiness, and diaphoresis, commonly triggered by diabetic medications, skipped meals, or insulinoma.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does my fasting glucose reading suggest prediabetes or diabetes?
- Should we confirm with an HbA1c test?
