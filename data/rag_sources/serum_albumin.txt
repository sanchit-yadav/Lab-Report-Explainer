What the Test Measures:
Albumin is the most abundant circulating plasma protein produced by the liver, responsible for maintaining colloid oncotic pressure and transporting hormones and medications.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
Elevated albumin is almost exclusively caused by acute hemoconcentration and dehydration.

What Low Values Mean:
Low albumin (hypoalbuminemia) indicates chronic liver failure (reduced synthesis), nephrotic syndrome (urinary loss), severe malnutrition, or acute systemic inflammatory states.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does my low albumin indicate a synthetic liver issue or protein loss through the kidneys?
- Do I have fluid retention or edema related to this result?
