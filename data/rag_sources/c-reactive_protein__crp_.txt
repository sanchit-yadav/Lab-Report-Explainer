What the Test Measures:
C-reactive protein is an acute-phase reactant synthesized by the liver in response to inflammatory cytokines.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
High CRP indicates acute or chronic inflammation. Markedly high levels (>10 mg/L) often point toward severe bacterial infections, major physical trauma, or acute flare-ups of chronic inflammatory conditions.

What Low Values Mean:
Low or undetectable CRP is normal and indicates an absence of significant systemic inflammation.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does this CRP elevation correlate with recent illness or symptoms?
- Does this reflect cardiovascular risk or an inflammatory flare?
