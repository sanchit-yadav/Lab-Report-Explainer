What the Test Measures:
MCV measures the average physical size of your red blood cells, categorizing anemias into microcytic, normocytic, or macrocytic.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
High MCV (macrocytosis) indicates enlarged red blood cells, commonly caused by Vitamin B12 deficiency, folate deficiency, liver dysfunction, excessive alcohol consumption, or thyroid disorders.

What Low Values Mean:
Low MCV (microcytosis) indicates smaller-than-normal red cells, most typically due to iron deficiency anemia or thalassemia trait.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does my cell size point toward iron deficiency or vitamin B12/folate deficiency?
- Should we run a ferritin or hemoglobin electrophoresis panel?
