What the Test Measures:
Total T3 measures both bound and free forms of triiodothyronine, the most biologically potent thyroid hormone.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
Elevated T3 is frequently seen in hyperthyroidism and T3 thyrotoxicosis.

What Low Values Mean:
Low T3 may indicate non-thyroidal systemic illness (euthyroid sick syndrome) or severe hypothyroidism.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Is a Total T3 or Free T3 test more informative for my clinical situation?
