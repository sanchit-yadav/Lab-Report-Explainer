What the Test Measures:
BUN measures the amount of nitrogen in your blood that comes from the breakdown of dietary proteins in the liver.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
Elevated BUN occurs in kidney disease, severe dehydration, congestive heart failure, gastrointestinal bleeding, or very high protein consumption.

What Low Values Mean:
Low BUN can be seen in severe liver disease, malnutrition, low-protein diets, or fluid overload.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- What is my BUN-to-creatinine ratio, and does it suggest dehydration or kidney parenchymal injury?
- Do I need to modify my fluid or protein intake?
