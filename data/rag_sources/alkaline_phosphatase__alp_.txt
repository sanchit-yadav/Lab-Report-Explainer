What the Test Measures:
Alkaline phosphatase is an enzyme found particularly in the cells lining bile ducts in the liver and in active bone tissue.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
High ALP occurs in biliary obstruction (cholestasis, gallstones), bone disorders (Paget's disease, healing fractures, bone metastases), or during normal pregnancy and childhood growth spurts.

What Low Values Mean:
Low ALP is uncommon but may occur in severe zinc deficiency, malnutrition, or hypophosphatasia.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does my elevated ALP originate from liver/biliary pathways or bone metabolism?
- Should we run a GGT test to differentiate between liver and bone origin?
