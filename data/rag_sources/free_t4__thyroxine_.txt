What the Test Measures:
Free T4 measures the unbound, metabolically active form of thyroxine circulating in your blood, unaffected by changes in carrier protein concentrations.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
High Free T4 indicates hyperthyroidism (such as Graves' disease, toxic multinodular goiter, or thyroiditis).

What Low Values Mean:
Low Free T4 indicates clinical hypothyroidism when accompanied by high TSH, or central hypothyroidism if TSH is normal or low.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- How does my Free T4 correlate with my TSH level?
- Does this result explain symptoms like fatigue, heart palpitations, or weight changes?
