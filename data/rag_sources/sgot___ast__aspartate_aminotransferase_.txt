What the Test Measures:
AST is an enzyme found in high concentrations in hepatocytes, cardiac myocytes, and skeletal muscle tissue. It leaks into the bloodstream when these cells sustain cellular injury.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
Elevated AST indicates liver inflammation (viral hepatitis, fatty liver, alcoholic liver disease), drug-induced hepatotoxicity, or strenuous physical exertion and muscle damage.

What Low Values Mean:
Low AST is normal and healthy.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- How does my AST compare to my ALT level (AST/ALT ratio)?
- Could any prescription drugs, herbal remedies, or alcohol be contributing?
