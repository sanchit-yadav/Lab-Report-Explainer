What the Test Measures:
Serum iron measures circulating iron bound to transferrin, while Ferritin measures the total cellular iron stores in your body.

What Low Values Mean:
Low iron and ferritin signify depleted iron stores and iron-deficiency anemia, often from menstrual loss, GI bleeding, or low dietary intake.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
Elevated iron and ferritin can point toward hemochromatosis (iron overload), repeated blood transfusions, or chronic inflammatory conditions (where ferritin acts as an acute-phase reactant).

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Do my ferritin and transferrin saturation levels confirm iron deficiency?
- Should we investigate potential occult gastrointestinal blood loss?
