What the Test Measures:
White blood cells (leukocytes) are a vital component of your immune system that defend against bacterial, viral, fungal, and parasitic infections, and play a key role in inflammatory responses.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
An elevated WBC count (leukocytosis) most commonly indicates an active bacterial or viral infection, tissue damage, severe physical stress, inflammatory conditions (e.g. rheumatoid arthritis), smoking, or, less commonly, bone marrow disorders.

What Low Values Mean:
A low WBC count (leukopenia) suggests an increased susceptibility to infection. Potential causes include viral infections that suppress bone marrow, autoimmune disorders (like lupus), bone marrow damage, or severe overwhelming infections.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does my elevated or reduced white count suggest an active or resolving infection?
- Would a differential count (neutrophils, lymphocytes) help identify the cause?
- Are any of my current medications contributing to this result?
