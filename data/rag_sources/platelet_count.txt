What the Test Measures:
Platelets (thrombocytes) are small, disc-shaped blood cell fragments essential for normal blood clotting and wound healing by clumping together to form plugs at injury sites.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
A high platelet count (thrombocytosis) can occur as a reactive response to acute infection, inflammation, tissue trauma, recent surgery, iron deficiency, or primary myeloproliferative disorders.

What Low Values Mean:
A low platelet count (thrombocytopenia) raises the risk of easy bruising and prolonged bleeding. It may stem from viral infections, autoimmune platelet destruction (ITP), splenic sequestration, or medications.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Do I need to be cautious about bleeding or bruising given my platelet count?
- Should we review my medications or over-the-counter pain relievers?
- When should we perform a follow-up complete blood count?
