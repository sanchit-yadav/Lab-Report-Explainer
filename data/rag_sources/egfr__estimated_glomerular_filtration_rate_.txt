What the Test Measures:
eGFR is the primary mathematical indicator of renal function, calculated from serum creatinine, age, and sex to estimate milliliters of blood filtered by the glomeruli per minute.

What Normal / Adequate Values Mean:
An eGFR of 60 or higher is generally considered in the normal or adequate range for adults.

What Reduced / Low Values Mean:
An eGFR below 60 for three months or longer indicates chronic kidney disease. A sudden decrease suggests acute kidney injury.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- What stage of kidney function does my eGFR correspond to?
- What blood pressure and glycemic targets should I maintain to protect my kidneys?
