What the Test Measures:
Uric acid is a waste byproduct formed when the body metabolizes purines, organic compounds found in bodily cells and foods like red meat, organ meats, seafood, and alcohol.

What Elevated / High Values Mean:
High uric acid (hyperuricemia) can crystallize in joints causing acute gouty arthritis, or form uric acid kidney stones. It can be caused by genetic predisposition, renal impairment, alcohol use, or metabolic syndrome.

What Low Values Mean:
Low uric acid is relatively uncommon and generally benign.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor:
- Does my uric acid level place me at risk for gout or kidney stones?
- What dietary adjustments (purine reduction, hydration) do you recommend?
